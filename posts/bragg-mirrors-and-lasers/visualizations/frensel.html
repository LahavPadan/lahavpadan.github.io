Successive interface reflections arrive in phase at \(k=k_B\)

Write \(k\) for the illumination wavenumber and \(k_B\) for the Bragg wavenumber. At \(k=k_B\), reflection 1 picks up phase \(\pi\) at the \(\mathrm{air}|H\) interface—the low-to-high sign change. Reflection 2 picks up phase \(\pi\) from the round trip through the quarter-wave \(H\) layer. The two returned fields add.

\(\phi_{\mathrm{return}}=\phi_{\mathrm{Fresnel}}+\phi_{\mathrm{prop}}\pmod{2\pi}\)
